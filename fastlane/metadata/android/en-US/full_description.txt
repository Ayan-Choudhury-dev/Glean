Glean is a local-first pantry tracker. Keep track of what is in your kitchen,
know what is running low, and build a shopping list - all stored on your device,
with no account and no server.

FEATURES

Pantry inventory
Track items with quantities, categories, storage locations and barcodes.
Adjust quantities inline as you use or restock things.

Shopping list
Add what you need to buy, then update your pantry in one tap when you get
home. Purchased items restock the matching inventory entries.

Recent activity
See what was used and restocked recently, per item.

Barcode scanning
Scan a product barcode with the camera when adding an item.

Import and export
Bring an existing list in as CSV, and export your inventory as CSV to back it
up or move it to another device.

Works offline
Everything lives in a local SQLite database on the phone. No internet
connection is required, there is no sign-in, and nothing is uploaded.

Optional self-hosted sync
If you want the same pantry on more than one device, you can point the app at
your own server running the bundled Docker setup. This is entirely optional;
the app works fully without it.

PRIVACY

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays on your device unless you
choose to connect your own server.
